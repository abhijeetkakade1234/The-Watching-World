# 🤖 AI System (OpenClaw)

## 🧠 Role

AI is not just an opponent.
It acts as a **dynamic villain controlling the world**.

---

## 🎯 Capabilities (MVP)

- Place traps
- Block paths
- React to player movement

---

## 🔥 Advanced Capabilities

- Predict player path
- Spawn enemies
- Create environmental effects

---

## ⚡ AI Actions

- Trap placement
- Area debuffs (slow, poison)
- Ambush zones

---

## 🧠 Learning System (Future)

AI learns:

- player movement patterns
- preferred paths
- behavior under pressure

---

## 💡 Goal

Make AI feel like:

> “Something intelligent is watching and reacting to me.”
