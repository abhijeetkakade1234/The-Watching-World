# ⚙️ Gameplay Mechanics

## 👤 Player Actions

- Move (free path-based movement)
- Attack (basic combat)
- Use ability (dash, reveal, shield)

---

## 🎒 Resources

- Coins
- Energy
- Items

---

## ⚔️ Weapons

- Basic melee (MVP)
- Advanced weapons (future)

---

## 🔥 Traps (AI-controlled)

- Poison zones
- Slow areas
- Push-back triggers

---

## ⚡ Power-Ups

- Speed boost
- Trap reveal
- Shield

---

## 🧭 Map Design

- Multiple paths
- Risk vs safe routes
- Hidden shortcuts

---

## 🎯 Win Condition

- Reach goal
- Complete objective
- Survive AI attacks
